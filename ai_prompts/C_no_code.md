# C: what you can and can't use AI for

- ✅ **OK:** Canva Magic Design, slide wording, icons, rehearsing your demo lines, making the fake shop names/products sound real.
- ❌ **Not OK:** working out the correct answers for the BENCHMARK tab. Do these by hand with Sheets formulas or a calculator. They're how we check the AI, so they can't come from an AI.
- ❌ **Not OK:** editing anything in GitHub. You only work in the Google Sheet and Canva. Daniel exports the sheet into the repo.

## Prompt for making fake data rows (optional, ChatGPT free / Gemini)
```
Give me 30 rows of fake shop orders as a table with columns:
order_id, order_date (YYYY-MM-DD, Jan to Mar 2024), customer_id (C1 to C20), amount (50 to 2000), currency (USD).
Plain table only, no explanation.
```
Then plant the traps yourself (copy a row to make a duplicate, blank an amount, change some currencies to EUR, write a date as 03/04/2024) and write each one in the TRAPS tab with its row number.
